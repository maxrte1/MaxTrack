About MaxTrack

A simple, browser-based study planner built for students who juggle several subjects and deadlines.

The problem we are solving

Students manage assignments, labs and exams across many subjects, but their deadlines are usually scattered across notes, chat groups and memory. This leads to missed deadlines and poor prioritisation. MaxTrack gives them one place to organise tasks by subject and priority, spot overdue work and see their progress.

What MaxTrack does

Task management

Add, edit, delete and complete tasks with validated input.

Filter and search

Narrow the list by subject, priority or status, or search by title.

Deadline tracking

Overdue and due-soon tasks are highlighted automatically.

Progress dashboard

See completed tasks and progress for each subject on the home page.

Built with

  • HTML5: semantic structure and accessible forms
  • CSS3: Flexbox, Grid and a responsive layout
  • JavaScript: DOM manipulation, event handling, validation and localStorage

Your tasks are saved only in your own browser. Clearing the browser data removes them.
